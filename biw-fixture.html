BIW Fixture Design & Drafting

BIW (Body-in-White) fixture design and drafting involves creating devices that accurately hold and position automotive body components during assembly and welding, ensuring repeatability and quality, with outputs including 3D models, 2D drawings and BOMs.

What is BIW Fixture Design?

BIW fixtures are essential for holding and positioning vehicle body components during the welding and assembly process. The design process considers part geometry, welding sequence, clamping requirements, safety and maintenance.

Drafting and Outputs

Our work provides detailed 2D drawings, 3D models, bills of materials, manufacturing data, and review-ready documentation.
