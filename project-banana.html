Our Best Project

Reverse Engineering of Automated Banana Fiber Extractor for Jothiees Engineering

In collaboration with Jothiees Engineering, we undertook a meticulous teardown and analysis of their automated banana fiber extractor machine, then generated detailed technical drawings for the entire system.

Project Highlights

Teardown and assessment, precision measurements, and 2D technical drawings created complete documentation for future reference and production.

Outcome

The project provided deeper machine insight, comprehensive manufacturing documentation, and a clear foundation for future improvements.
